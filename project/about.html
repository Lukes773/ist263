Outside of Academics

Outside of academics, I have a wide range of hobbies. I ran cross country in high school and still love running outdoors when the weather is nice, and lifting has become a big part of my routine as well. I enjoy walking, cooking, and when my schedule allows, playing video games with friends. I live in Rochester, NY, not too far from Syracuse University, and when I'm home you will often find me hanging out with my dogs or playing cards with my dad.

Travel & Studying Abroad

I also love traveling and seeing the world. Last fall I studied abroad in London and made it a priority to see as much of the surrounding countries as I could with the limited time I had there, which ended up being one of my favorite experiences so far. Between school, training, and travel, I try to make sure I'm always learning something new, whether that's in a classroom or not.
